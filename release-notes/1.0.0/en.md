DewShot is a native Mac menu bar app for screenshots and screen recordings. Capture with a keyboard shortcut, then copy, save, or open the result from the corner preview.

- Capture a region, a window, or the full screen. Take scrolling screenshots of long pages and see the stitched result as you scroll.
- Record a selected area as MP4 or GIF, with optional pointer, click, and keystroke overlays.
- Recognize text from screenshots on your Mac and copy all the text or selected blocks.
- Pin screenshots to the desktop or drag them into other apps, open screenshots or recordings in the default app, and find recent captures in Capture History.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Screenshots and recordings are processed on your Mac, and no account is required. You can turn off usage statistics and diagnostics on the welcome screen or in Settings.
