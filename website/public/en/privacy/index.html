Privacy policy

Updated November 24, 2026. This policy applies to the DewShot macOS app and this website.

Captures and local history

DewShot requires no account. Screenshots, recordings, and text recognition are processed on your Mac. Captures can be stored in local history, on the clipboard, or in a folder you choose. You can clear cached captures in History and delete saved files in Finder. Other apps or synced folders may handle content you share with them according to their settings.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

When sharing is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives analytics associated with the app installation, including permission status during setup, features used, operation results, and timings. Sentry receives crash diagnostics and reports of certain failed operations, such as error categories and call stacks. We use this information to understand feature usage, diagnose problems, and improve performance.

DewShot does not send either service screenshots, recordings, recognized text, keystroke content, clipboard content, window titles, or saved file names and paths.

Turning sharing off

The App Store and TestFlight welcome screens preselect usage and diagnostics sharing. You can turn it off before selecting Get Started or at any time in Settings → Privacy & Permissions. Turning it off stops collection and clears pending local diagnostic data. Previously delivered data remains subject to the retention settings of the respective service.

Website and support email

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes connection information such as IP addresses to serve pages and secure the network. The website does not embed Google Analytics or Sentry. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond.

Privacy questions and deletion requests

To ask about retention or request deletion of data relating to you, email deweyou97@gmail.com and describe the data involved. DewShot has no accounts and does not use email addresses as analytics identifiers, so an email address alone may not identify diagnostic records for a particular installation.
